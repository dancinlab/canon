## Why it matters — why knee cartilage is uniquely hard

For tissues like **gums, hair and retina**, senescent-cell clearance ([[SENOLYX-AG]]) opens the
regeneration gate on its own. Those tissues have **blood vessels**, so blood flow delivers both the drug and
the raw materials for building cells.

But **knee cartilage has no blood vessels (avascular, acellular)**. So:

```
The house-repair analogy
───────────────
Vascular tissue = a neighbourhood with roads  → one cleaning truck finishes the job
Knee cartilage  = an island with no road      → the cleaning truck cannot get in (δ),
                                                and no workers or materials arrive (A)
                                                → clearance alone can never fix it
```

What we **proved mathematically**: senescent-cell clearance alone (with any delivery method whatsoever)
**fundamentally cannot close** the knee-cartilage treatment gate (ceiling 0.755 < required 0.90). Of the four
treatments, knee cartilage is the sole exception.
